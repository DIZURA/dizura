# Option photos — art direction

Source of truth for the look of every option card in the Visual Character builder
(`public/influencer/options/<group>/<option>.webp`, generated by `npm run photos:options`).
Owner's references (2026-10-23): Higgsfield AI Influencer builder cards. We follow the **style**,
never their images — every photo is generated fresh by our own account with our own prompts.

## The look
- **Pure white seamless background**, high-key soft studio light, no shadows on the backdrop,
  subject centered. Reads like a cut-out on a white card.
- **Square** framing (1:1). Head-and-shoulders for face/identity/hair/accessory groups;
  **full body, feet visible, standing straight, facing camera** for build and style groups.
- **Quirky, characterful casting** — editorial fashion meets internet-meme humour:
  exaggerated hairstyles (blunt bobs, towering pompadours, flat-tops, beehives, mullets,
  mohawks), statement glasses, deadpan or wildly expressive faces (tongue out, wide eyes,
  open-mouth roar), retro outfits (sweater vests, bow ties, sailor collars, tracksuits).
- **Animals with human hairstyles** are the signature: a frog with a flat-top, a cat with an
  auburn pompadour, a pug with a tall black flat-top, a capybara with a crew cut, a goose with a
  black bob. Photoreal fur/feathers/skin, comedic expression.
- **Body builds are played for humour but kind**: very slim in an oversized suit, curvy in a
  polka-dot dress with a beehive, heavy-set in a sweater vest and bow tie, hyper-muscular in a
  too-tight tweed suit, a surreal one (e.g. a centaur-style body in jeans).
- **DIZURA flavour on top**: Gulf Arab, Levantine and North African faces, hijab, abaya, thobe and
  ghutra, majlis-era retro — mixed with the quirky casting, never mocking culture or religion.

## Rules
- **Never reproduce a reference person.** References set the *style* only; every card gets its own
  casting (different face, hair, colours, outfit and pose). Prompts describe our own characters,
  never the people in the owner's screenshots.
- Fictional people only — no celebrities, no real individuals, no brand logos or text.
- Respectful: humour comes from styling and expression, never from ethnicity, religion,
  disability or body-shaming.
- Same subject within a group where comparison matters (Emotion, Look): one model, only the
  option changes.
- UI: photo cards are white tiles with the label underneath (see `CharacterBuilder.tsx`);
  options without a photo fall back to DIZURA glyph/placeholder art.

## Regenerating
`npm run photos:options -- --dry` prints every prompt. `-- --force` regenerates all,
`-- --only=animal,build` limits groups. Prompts live in `scripts/generate-option-photos.mjs`
(`STYLE`, `PROMPTS`); bump `VERSION` there when the art direction changes.
